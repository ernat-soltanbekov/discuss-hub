How these insights work

This is transparent, rule-based analysis over the forum’s own data. Each occurrence of an English keyword counts, regardless of case. Only whole words in post content affect sentiment.

Positive: great, love, excellent, amazing, helpful, good, thanks, awesome.
Negative: bad, hate, terrible, awful, horrible, wrong, broken, useless.

Score = positive matches − negative matches. Above zero is Positive; below zero is Negative; zero is Neutral. “Not good” is Positive under this simple rule. Sarcasm, context and other languages are not interpreted. Engagement measures reactions, not quality or agreement.

All posts contribute to mood. Category counts include each selected category. Activity counts posts and comments, not reactions. Equal activity totals sort by name; equal reaction totals sort newest first. Every recent post can be reached with pagination.
